Reading List

Books from my reading list, from neuroscience and statistics to startups and fiction. The years refer to when I read them; the ratings are personal.

§ 01

books

2026
50 Great Short Stories
Edited by Milton Crane
★ 4.7 / 5
A collection of fifty short stories across different writers and styles. A useful way to explore the form in small doses.
2025
Zero to One: Notes on Startups
Peter Thiel
★ 4.5 / 5
A case for building something distinctive rather than competing only by imitation. The central question is what a new company can do that others cannot.
2025
Lost and Founder: A Painfully Honest Field Guide to the Startup World
Rand Fishkin
★ 4.2 / 5
Fishkin’s account of building a company, with attention to fundraising, growth, and the difficult choices behind startup success stories.
2025
Brain: An Owner's Guide (The Body Literacy Library)
Elizabeth R. Ricker
★ 4.5 / 5
An accessible introduction to the brain. I liked the connection between the science and everyday questions.
2025
Secrets of Sand Hill Road: Venture Capital and How to Get It
Scott Kupor
★ 4.4 / 5
An introduction to how venture capital works, including how investors evaluate companies and how financing decisions affect founders.
2025
The Parasitic Mind: How Infectious Ideas Are Killing Common Sense
Gad Saad
★ 4.1 / 5
Saad’s argument about ideology, intellectual conformity, and open debate, presented through psychology and cultural commentary.
2025
Mastery
Robert Greene
★ 4.5 / 5
Greene explores the development of expertise through examples of apprenticeship, practice, and independent work.
2024
Sleep Tight
J.H. Markert
★ 4.5 / 5
A dark thriller built around memory and trauma. I found the atmosphere and the character of Father Silence especially unsettling.
2023
The Deep History of Ourselves
Joseph LeDoux
★ 4.8 / 5
LeDoux connects the evolution of survival mechanisms with questions about behavior and consciousness. I used figures from the book in my Yale PhD defense.
2023
fMRI
Peter Bandettini
★ 4.0 / 5
An introduction to functional MRI, its development, and the questions it can help researchers investigate.
2022
The Entangled Brain: How Perception, Cognition, and Emotion Are Woven Together
Luiz Pessoa
★ 4.6 / 5
I liked the challenge to treating perception, cognition, and emotion as separate systems. The book emphasizes their interdependence.
2022
Causal Inference
Paul R. Rosenbaum
★ 4.1 / 5
An introduction to causal reasoning, including the challenge of distinguishing associations from causal effects in observational data.
